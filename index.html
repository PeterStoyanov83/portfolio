How I work

  1. Listen. What costs you time or money today? I start from your business, not from a technology.
  2. Lay out the parts. A short written plan you can read, with the risky parts named up front.
  3. Build and test. Anything that touches money, customers or personal data gets tests first.
  4. Hand over. Every project ends with a handoff document: what's built, why, and what's left. You own the code.

AI integration & automation · my own product, in use

Forge

A proactive AI assistant I built for my own workshop: tasks, calendar, email and voice in Bulgarian, English and Turkish. I'm now putting it to work as Forge Agency, which drafts and publishes Instagram and Facebook posts for small businesses.

Models
3-tier routing: local Ollama, then Claude Haiku, then Claude Sonnet
Cost
About 70% lower AI spend after prompt caching and context work
Channels
Instagram and Facebook through the Meta Graph API, one-click connect
Stack
FastAPI, PostgreSQL, React, background workers, 345 tests
  • The guardrail fails closed. If a check errors out, the draft counts as flagged, never as passed.
  • Comments from the internet are fenced off as untrusted text, so a planted instruction can't talk the guardrail into approving itself.
  • Every post and every reply waits for a human, then a publish window (24 hours by default). An append-only log records what went out and who approved it.

Backend & payments · najivo.bg, pre-launch

НаЖиво.бг

Ticketing for Bulgarian cultural venues. Venues sell seats, buyers pay once, and Stripe Connect splits the money between the venue and the platform. The hard part is making every euro add up, including after a refund.

Payments
Stripe Connect destination charges, refunds with transfer reversal
Platform
FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Redis seat locking, React, 2FA, invoices
Money
Decimal everywhere, ROUND_HALF_UP, integer cents at the API
  • The fee is max(percent × total + fixed, minimum), because a plain percentage loses money on cheap tickets once card processing is paid.
  • Refunds call the provider first, with an idempotency key. Seat release and emails run after, and a failure there can never undo a refund.
  • Connect refunds reverse the transfer to the venue. Found in end-to-end testing, when two test refunds drained the platform balance.

Website & web app · my own venture, live

Kayu

A marketplace for exotic tonewood. Producers in Asia, Africa and South America list their wood; luthiers in Europe and the US buy it directly. The kind of wood this fretboard is made from.

Apps
FastAPI backend, React Native / Expo for iOS, Android and web
Languages
7: English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Indonesian, Bulgarian
  • Translations are typed against English: a missing key in an existing language fails the build, while a new language falls back to English instead of blocking release.
  • The 22,000 words of legal terms stay human-written. They're what a dispute would be read against, so they aren't machine-translated.
